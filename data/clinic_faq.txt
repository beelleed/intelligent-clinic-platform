Clinic Information

The clinic is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and from 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

First-time patients should arrive 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

Appointments can be booked online or by calling the clinic during business hours.

Patients who need to cancel or reschedule an appointment should contact the clinic before their scheduled appointment time.

Surgery Schedule

When the doctor is performing a procedure, the clinic staff can update the doctor's status to "In Surgery."

A standard procedure is initially estimated to take 90 minutes.

If the procedure takes longer than expected, clinic staff can extend the estimated completion time by 15, 30, or 60 minutes.

The estimated completion time is only an approximation and may change depending on the actual procedure.

Waiting Status

The clinic website displays the number currently being seen.

Clinic staff update the current number as patients complete their visits.

Patients can check the clinic website remotely to view the latest consultation progress.
