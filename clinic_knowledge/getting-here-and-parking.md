# Getting Here and Parking

The demo clinic provides free, designated visitor parking at each clinic
location during appointments. Parking spaces are limited, available on a
first-come, first-served basis, and cannot be reserved through the website.

Accessible parking spaces are located near the main entrance. A passenger
drop-off and pick-up area is also available by the entrance for visitors who
need additional mobility assistance.

Visitors should allow extra time to park and follow the signs posted at their
scheduled clinic location. The public assistant does not have live parking-space
occupancy information.
