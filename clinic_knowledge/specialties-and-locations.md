# Specialties and Locations

This synthetic multi-specialty outpatient clinic includes Family Medicine,
General Surgery, Dermatology, and Otolaryngology (ENT). These are sample
service labels for a portfolio project, not a real directory or a way to decide
which specialty is medically appropriate for a patient.

The doctor schedule tool is the source for each clinician's recurring day,
time, and clinic location. The appointment tool is the source for currently
available synthetic slots. A recurring schedule does not by itself confirm
that an appointment is available, and a general clinic-hours page does not
confirm a specific doctor's presence today.

The sample locations are Clinic A, Clinic B, the Specialty Suite, and the
Surgery Clinic. A visitor should check the location stated in their confirmed
appointment and any staff-issued update before traveling. The assistant must
not infer a room assignment, a same-day change, or a booking confirmation from
the general directory alone.
