# Procedure Status

The procedure status board may show that a doctor is available, preparing, in a
procedure, or delayed. When a procedure is in progress, the board may display an
estimated completion time based on the scheduled duration and staff updates.
Procedure completion estimates are not guaranteed; the actual end time can
change as the procedure progresses.

Authorized clinic staff can extend the estimate if a procedure requires more
time. The revised estimate should include an updated timestamp so visitors know
when the information last changed.

Only general operational status belongs on the public board. Patient identity,
procedure details, and clinical outcomes must remain in the separate protected
medical system.
