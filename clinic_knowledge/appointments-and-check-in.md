# Appointments and Check-In

Patients should arrive 15 minutes before a scheduled appointment to allow time
for check-in. Late arrivals may need to be rescheduled when the remaining visit
time would not be sufficient for safe care.

Appointment requests may be submitted online, but a request is not confirmed
until the clinic sends a confirmation. The public website must not collect or
display detailed medical records, diagnoses, or government health-card data.

To cancel or reschedule, contact the clinic as early as possible so the time can
be offered to another patient.
