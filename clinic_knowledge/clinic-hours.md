# Clinic Hours

The demo clinic is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and
from 2:00 PM to 6:00 PM Pacific Time. The clinic is closed from 12:00 PM to
2:00 PM and is normally closed on weekends and public holidays.

Doctor availability can differ from general clinic hours. Visitors should use
the doctor schedule or appointment tools when they need a specific clinician's
availability.
